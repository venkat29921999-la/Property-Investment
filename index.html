About Stackly

A smaller book of deals, held for longer.

Most investment firms grow their fee income by growing their deal count. We do the opposite — we underwrite fewer buildings, hold them longer, and put our own capital in every acquisition alongside our partners.

That discipline shows up in the numbers: a 17‑year average hold period against an industry norm closer to five, and a loss ratio under one percent across two market cycles. Every property in the portfolio is walked, inspected and modelled by our own team before it's ever offered to an investor.

Owner‑operator underwriting

How an allocation works

Five steps, roughly six weeks.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We review your goals, timeline and existing exposure to real assets.

  2. 02

    Allocation design

    We match you to specific holdings or a pooled vehicle based on risk and liquidity needs.

  3. 03

    Underwriting review

    You receive the same inspection, title and financial package our committee reviewed.

  4. 04

    Closing

    Capital is placed through escrow with independent title and legal counsel.

  5. 05

    Ongoing reporting

    Quarterly statements, occupancy data and a standing line to your asset manager.
